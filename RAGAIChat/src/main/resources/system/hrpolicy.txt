# Human Resources Handbook (Test Document)

## Section 1: Company Policies
- Equal Employment Opportunity: The company provides equal opportunities regardless of race, gender, age, or disability.
- Anti-Harassment Policy: Zero tolerance for harassment in any form.
- Code of Conduct: Employees must maintain professionalism and integrity at all times.

## Section 2: Leave & Attendance
- Annual Leave: 20 days per year, accrued monthly.
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year, requires medical certificate for absences longer than 3 days.
- Attendance Policy: Employees must log working hours via the HR portal.

## Section 3: Compensation & Benefits
- Payroll: Salaries are processed on the 30th of each month.
- Health Insurance: Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision.
- Retirement Plan: Company contributes 5% of base salary to provident fund.

## Section 4: Performance Management
- Appraisals: Conducted annually in December.
- Promotions: Based on performance ratings and business needs.
- Training: Employees are encouraged to attend at least 2 skill development sessions per year.

## Section 5: Employee Relations
- Grievance Handling: Employees may raise concerns via HR helpdesk.
- Disciplinary Action: Progressive discipline policy (verbal warning → written warning → termination).
- Exit Process: Includes clearance, final settlement, and exit interview.

## Section 6: Safety & Compliance
- Workplace Safety: Employees must follow safety protocols and report hazards.
- Compliance: Adherence to labor laws and data protection regulations.
- Confidentiality: Sensitive company information must not be shared externally.

---

### Sample Employee Guidelines
1. Dress Code: Business casual from Monday to Thursday, casual on Friday.
2. Remote Work: Allowed up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
3. Travel Policy: Expenses reimbursed within 15 days of submission.
